Most accounts of Athens are written in July, when it is loud, hot and full. I
arrived in February, when it is none of those things — and I have never quite
recovered from what I found.

## Off-peak Athens

Winter here is the city you can actually live in: the mornings sharp and
clear, the museums quiet, the tables easy. The famous energy is still there,
it is simply shared with you instead of performed for strangers.

## The market is the calendar

In the off-season you stop judging the weather by forecasts and start reading
it in the market — what the naviera hauled in, what is in season, what the
bakers are turning out. You build a week around produce, not plans.

## Why it matters

A nomad should not see Athens. A nomad should *inhabit* its shoulder seasons,
when the city is at face value rather than at full volume. That is the only
light in which a place tells you the truth about itself.
