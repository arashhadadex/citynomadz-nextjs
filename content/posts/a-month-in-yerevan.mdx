I went to Yerevan expecting an itinerary. It gave me, instead, a routine.

The first week is tourism — the cascade, the etched stone, the postcard
angles. The second and third weeks are the real thing: the same baker each
morning, the same table in the same café, the mountain that stops being a
backdrop and becomes a neighbour.

## The ledger

Numbers first, dated and honest, rounded to the nearest hundred.

- **Rent** — a one-bed in the centre, tracked across the full stay.
- **Food** — market runs and home cooking, with the occasional meal out.
- **Mobile & wifi** — a prepay that lasted the whole stretch without a hitch.
- **All-in** — the monthly reality behind the field notes.

## It grows on you

Nobody is eased into a city by force. You ease in through repetition — the
same walk, the same baker, the same view. By the third month the city has
stopped *arriving* and started *surrounding*.

That is the whole point of City Nomadz: not a review, but a friendship.
